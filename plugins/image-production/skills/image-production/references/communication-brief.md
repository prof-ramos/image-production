# Adaptive briefing for communication pieces

Apply this reference to invitations, social posts, stories, carousels, ads,
posters and other pieces intended to communicate an offer or message. It applies
without a card. For unrelated image tasks (a cutout, a background edit, an
illustration), clarify only decisions needed for that task; do not impose a
marketing questionnaire.

## Readiness before production

1. Read the conversation and attachments. Separate supplied facts and exact copy,
   context for the exercise, requested omissions, decisions delegated to you and
   unresolved information. Keep answers across turns; do not restart the briefing.
2. Check the applicable areas below. An area is resolved by information already
   supplied, explicit omission/absence, a clear decision delegated by the user,
   or being irrelevant to the requested piece. Silence is not delegation. Infer
   only what the request makes unambiguous; never fill missing factual details.
3. Ask one concise, grouped message containing only unresolved decisions needed
   to make this piece. Acknowledge known information briefly. Offer relevant style
   directions when style remains open. Accept a single freeform answer and files;
   do not require the user to fill a form or restate known data.
4. If drafting copy is useful, propose it in text with supplied facts only, clearly
   labelled as a proposal, and obtain approval of the exact wording before image
   production. Combine that approval request with remaining questions where possible.
   A later change to proposed wording requires approval of that changed wording.
5. Once applicable decisions and artwork copy are resolved, proceed under the
   existing tool and review workflow. The complete brief itself is sufficient:
   do not add a ritual confirmation or ask permission to generate again.

Until step 5, do not call the image tool, hand off generation, reserve an attempt
with `prepare`, or route to standalone `$imagegen` to skip these questions.
Reading references and preparing a text proposal are allowed. This is a
conversational gate; the artifact helper does not verify business readiness.

## Applicable decisions

| Area | What to establish if missing | What avoids unnecessary questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offer/message | What is being communicated, exact names and principal message; hierarchy when several compete | Reuse the supplied subject; do not ask what the event is when already named |
| Channel and delivery | Where it will appear or be sent (e.g. feed, story, WhatsApp, email, print); needed deliverables and variants | Reuse supplied format/proportion. Choose production details from the agreed destination; ask only if the placement or delivery choice materially changes the piece |
| Audience and purpose | Who receives it, desired result and action (invite, save the date, confirm attendance, buy, contact) | A clearly stated “save the date for associates” resolves these; do not require a separate audience profile |
| Brand and materials | Whether to use a logo, brand rules, photos or prior artwork; request the relevant files | “No logo”, “use text only”, or permission to proceed without materials resolves absence. Do not keep requesting a kit or fabricate a logo, official colors or an identity |
| Facts and CTA | Approved date/time/place/terms/contact/link and call to action, only when needed for the chosen purpose | A save-the-date may intentionally omit time/address/RSVP. Ask whether absent details should be omitted when that affects usability; never invent them |
| Visual style | Existing reference, colors/fonts, preferred tone or creative delegation | Offer a few pertinent directions, e.g. institutional, relaxed, elegant/festive for an invitation. “Choose the style” resolves style, not audience, event facts or branding |
| Artwork copy | Exact approved wording, including whether a context note belongs on the image | Preserve final text verbatim. An idea authorizes a proposal, not silently treating new wording as final |

When multiple channels are requested, clarify the placements and whether distinct
versions or a shared image are wanted only if still ambiguous. Agree the delivery
set before reserving keys. Do not silently multiply the number of assets or promise
PDF/editable files that the raster workflow does not produce. Explain any requested
unsupported delivery format briefly and resolve that need before production.

Creative delegation can cover layout, style and proposed phrasing; it never supplies
missing dates, prices, benefits, endorsements, addresses or contacts. If the user
explicitly requests an immediate draft with the available facts, respect that scope:
use only those facts and the explicitly delegated decisions, omit unspecified facts,
and clarify any indispensable remaining ambiguity. Do not manufacture consent to new
copy: propose new wording for approval before generating; supplied exact copy needs
no extra approval.

“Fictional event”, “example” or “test” describes context unless the user expressly
requests that label as artwork text. Do not add it automatically. Ask whether it
belongs on the piece only when its intended use makes that distinction material.
A reference image supplies visual evidence, not automatically approved copy or facts.

## Conversation examples (guidance, not a fixed script)

**Incomplete ASOF invitation.** User: “Convite para o evento de fim de ano da ASOF,
dia 03/12 no Mundial (apenas evento fictício).”

Possible response:
“Já temos ASOF, confraternização de fim de ano, 03/12 e Mundial. Para preparar o
convite, falta definir:
1. Onde será publicado ou enviado e quais versões precisa?
2. Quem receberá e a intenção é convidar, reservar a data ou pedir confirmação?
3. Pode enviar o logo e alguma referência da ASOF, ou prefere seguir sem esses materiais?
4. Há horário, endereço ou contato que devem aparecer, ou usamos só data e local?
5. Prefere algo institucional, descontraído ou elegante e festivo?
Posso propor a redação para sua aprovação antes de gerar.”

Do not choose blue/gold, reserve an attempt, generate, or print “evento fictício”.
The known date and venue are not questions again.

**Partial reply.** User: “WhatsApp, associados, só reservar a data; sem logo.
Pode escolher o estilo. Não temos horário ainda.”

Retain all prior answers. Resolve delivery details only if material; omit time,
use no invented logo, choose a style without another style question. Propose
“Reserve a data / Confraternização de fim de ano da ASOF / 03/12 • Mundial” as
copy for approval. Do not generate while that new copy is awaiting approval.

**Approved complete brief.** User: “Aprovado esse texto. Uma imagem vertical 2:3
para WhatsApp, só PNG, associados, sem logo e sem RSVP; escolha o estilo.”

Proceed without a second approval question. The exact approved copy, channel,
delivery, purpose, audience, brand absence and delegated style are resolved.
Keep the exercise context out of the artwork.

**Complete initial request.** User provides Instagram feed, a single square PNG,
audience, offer, exact final copy and CTA, supplied logo and brand reference,
and an elegant style. Proceed; do not ask the seven areas as a checklist.

**Multiple channels.** “Instagram e WhatsApp” alone may leave feed versus story
and one versus several versions unresolved. Ask just those missing decisions
alongside any other actual gaps; preserve the common facts and approved copy.
